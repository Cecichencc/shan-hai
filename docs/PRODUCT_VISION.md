# Product vision — Shan Hai / 山海经

## Promise
Enter a delightful, three-dimensional interpretation of the Classic of Mountains and Seas. Explore stylized Chinese landscapes, meet strange mythological creatures, challenge them in short strategic battles, and invite them into your traveling team.

## Core loop (MVP target)
**Explore → Encounter → Battle → Recruit after victory / Restart expedition after defeat → Collection → Re-enter valley.**

A loss should currently restart the **expedition**, preserving prior permanent discoveries and recruited companions. This decision is provisional: the player has mentioned a full restart; test the less-punishing variant first, then revisit reset severity in playtesting.

## Player
A modern young traveler; brown short layered hair with **no hair bun**, simple white T-shirt, dark shorts, neutral sneakers, optional modest crossbody bag. 2.5–3-head-tall chibi proportions. Keep the small form easy to animate and recognizable from the exploration camera.

## Creature roster planned (only 帝江 in Phase 0)
帝江 (Dijiang), 九尾狐 (Nine-tailed Fox), 白泽 (Baize), 应龙 (Yinglong), 穷奇 (Qiongqi), 烛龙 (Zhulong), 精卫 (Jingwei).

Existing character sheets are a **stylistic reference**, not guaranteed mythologically canonical anatomy. Confirm original descriptions before model lock. The generated sheets sometimes contain inaccurate descriptions; do not reproduce invented claims as history.

## Design goals
1. Delightful, readable 3D motion on phone and desktop.
2. Mythological identity recognizable in creature silhouette and behavior.
3. Clear stakes without repetitive, grindy battles.
4. A small region feels alive through interactions, not through asset count.

## Not in Phase 0
Production hosting, a full world map simulation, combat, recruiting, leveling, advanced AI, dedicated animated GLB rigs, accounts, voice, multiplayer and cross-device sync.
