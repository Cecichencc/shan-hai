# 帝江 / DIJIANG — Immutable art and interaction contract

## Source-inspired fundamentals
Round, faceless, six-legged, four-winged, connected to music and dance in the received 山海经 tradition. Game personality must come from **movement**, rhythm, feather reactions, and sound—not from eyes or a smile.

## Locked appearance
- **ONE** large round, sack-like cream/ivory body.
- **NO face** whatsoever: zero eyes, nose, mouth, ears, brows or whiskers. The previous concept boards sometimes hallucinated faces; they are not to be followed.
- **SIX** independent short legs arranged as three pairs; cylindrical puffy legs with chunky hoof-like ends, *not feline toes/paws*.
- **FOUR** distinct orange/gold/cinnabar feather wings: paired left/right, upper/lower. Readable from front, back, side and top views.
- Slightly layered plush fur clumps, coral flame swirls; no expensive individual hairs.

## Procedural Phase 0 proxy
Body: ellipsoid, leg joints: rounded capsules, hoof tips: contrasting coral spheres, wings: five feather capsules per wing. The proportions are temporary and not a replacement for the approved orthographic character turnaround.

## Phase 2 production asset
`dijiang.glb`: named nodes `root`, `body`, `leg_L_front`, `leg_R_front`, `leg_L_mid`, `leg_R_mid`, `leg_L_back`, `leg_R_back`, `wing_L_upper`, `wing_R_upper`, `wing_L_lower`, `wing_R_lower`. Check mesh counts **by semantic nodes**, not visually approximate via generated imagery.

Animations: `idle` (gentle breath), `walk_six` (alternating tripods), `wing_flutter`, `notice_player`, `battle_idle`, `song_wind`, `take_hit`, `victory_dance`, `join_player`.

Acceptance: recognizable silhouette at phone size, no accidental extra limbs, six legs visibly taking turns, each of four wings moving independently; maintains 30 FPS goal on test phone once scene assets are optimized.
